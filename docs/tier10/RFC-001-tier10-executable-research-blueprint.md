# RFC-001: Tier 10 Executable Research Blueprint

## Summary

Tier 10 promotes civilizational research findings into tables, finite state machines, verification gates, simulation kernels, graph schemas, event topics, streaming jobs, APIs, and operational dashboards.

## Executable Mapping

Institutional evolution maps to `institutional_entities`, `institutional_mutations`, `institutional_lifecycle_events`, and `transitionInstitutionState`.

Meta-governance maps to constitution, amendment, constraint, invariant, conflict, resolution, and replay event tables plus rule compilation and amendment validation APIs.

Epistemic integrity maps to claim, evidence, truth, contradiction, and uncertainty registries plus `reconcileEvidence` and `reviseBelief`.

Knowledge preservation maps to artifact, archival tier, strategy, replication, decay, and retrieval tables plus durability, redundancy, and survivability scoring.

Alignment persistence maps to principles, policies, measurements, drift events, remediation, audit history, and the alignment drift detector.

Civilizational simulation maps to agent, institution, governance node, resource, environmental, trust, technology, conflict, SDT, historical calibration, technology evolution, strategic competition, and projection artifacts.

## Verification

Amendments are activation-blocked until TLA+, Alloy, SMT, simulation, and rollback gates pass. Governance decision events carry replay keys and aggregate sequence constraints.
