# ADR-001: Tier 10 Civilizational Governance Platform

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Tier 10 converts institutional evolution, meta-governance, epistemic integrity, preservation, alignment persistence, civilizational simulation, historical calibration, technology evolution, and strategic competition research into executable platform artifacts.

## Decision

KARTEX stores Tier 10 source of truth in additive Supabase tables, projects graph-native relations into Neo4j, publishes replayable lifecycle events through Kafka, verifies constitutional activation through TLA+/Alloy/SMT gates, and exposes deterministic TypeScript kernels through `/api/tier10/*`.

## Consequences

Every Tier 10 decision is replay-keyed, every amendment is activation-gated by formal verification and simulation, and every long-horizon projection remains advisory until governance approves downstream action.
