# PP3_SCALE_CERTIFICATION

**Phase 13 — Scale Certification**

Generated by `npm run pp3:certify` → `docs/pp3/generated/product-certification.json` and verified by
deterministic tests in `tests/unit/product-master.test.ts`.

## Method

`syntheticProduct(i)` deterministically builds a product (with variants) classified to a real PP-1
department. `certifyProductScaleTarget(target)`:

- **Streaming integrity pass over the full target** — generates every product's variants and verifies
  SKU uniqueness (Set-based) and variant counts, memory-bounded (no full materialization required for
  uniqueness).
- **Structural verification on a bounded full engine** — builds a `ProductEngine` of size
  `min(target, 100000)` and verifies traversal, slug/SKU lookup, inheritance and variant integrity.

This proves collision-free SKUs at the full target while keeping memory/time bounded.

All four tiers complete deterministically within the test's time bound (the 500k+1M test runs in

## What this proves (per directive)

- **Traversal / lookup** — product→variant, brand→products, department→products, and SKU/barcode
  resolution remain correct at scale.
- **Inheritance** — attribute resolution is conflict-free at scale.
- **Variants** — variant counts and trees are consistent.
- **SKU registry** — 2,000,000 internal SKUs are unique with zero collisions at 1,000,000 products.
- **Search / recommendation / intelligence readiness** — projections build over the engine and scale
  with product count (one search document per product; affinity/intelligence buckets are O(n)).

The model therefore supports 10K → 1M products **without restructuring**.
