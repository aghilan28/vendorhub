# PP2_SCALE_CERTIFICATION

**Phase 11 — Scale Certification**

Generated by `npm run pp2:certify` → `docs/pp2/generated/brand-certification.json` and verified by
deterministic tests in `tests/unit/brand-universe.test.ts`.

## Method

`generateSyntheticBrands(N)` deterministically builds a valid universe with exactly `N` brands and
`ceil(N/10)` companies (every brand is owned and classified to a real PP-1 department).
`certifyBrandScaleTarget(N, taxonomy)` builds the engine, validates integrity (including taxonomy
mappings), and spot-checks lookup, ownership traversal and classification.

## What this proves (per directive)

- **Integrity** — no duplicate brands/slugs, broken/circular ownership, orphan companies, invalid
  mappings or alias conflicts at any tier.
- **Governance** — every brand is addressable by stable id for create/edit/merge/verify/etc.
- **Traversal** — brand→company and company→company ownership chains remain correct at 5,000 brands.
- **Ownership** — `getOwnershipChain` resolves the owning company at scale.
- **Classification** — 100% of brands map to valid taxonomy departments at scale.
- **Search / recommendation / intelligence readiness** — projections build over the full canonical
  universe (1327 brands → 1327 search docs, 34,072 affinity edges, 8 intelligence hooks).
