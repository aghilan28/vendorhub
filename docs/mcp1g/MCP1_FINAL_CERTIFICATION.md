## What Was Learned

1. **Engine-first architecture** enables testing without infrastructure
2. **Degrade-safe design** allows development without live dependencies
3. **Deterministic engines** are superior to API-dependent intelligence
4. **Operations systems** are as critical as commerce systems
5. **Security must be built-in** from day one, not retrofitted
6. **Real validation requires real users** — no amount of testing substitutes for market contact
7. **Pilot playbooks matter** — the gap between "code works" and "business works" is operational, not technical
