# Seller Pilot Playbook

**Target:** 5-20 sellers in hyperlocal categories  
**Timeline:** Week 1-2 of pilot  

---

## Target Seller Profile

| Attribute | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| Type | Local retail store (grocery, vegetables, fruits, bakery, dairy, household) |
| Location | Within pilot geography (single city/neighborhood) |
| Products | Minimum 20 products with real images |
| Delivery | Capable of fulfilling within 5km radius |
| Technology | Smartphone with camera |
| Legal | Valid GSTIN or business registration |
| Banking | Bank account for receiving payouts |

---

## Onboarding Process

### Step 1: Recruitment (Days 1-3)
- Visit local stores in target area
- Explain value proposition: "Get online orders from nearby customers"
- Collect contact details of interested sellers
- Demonstrate the seller dashboard on phone

### Step 2: Registration (Days 3-5)
- Guide seller through `/seller-registration`
- Help complete 12-step onboarding wizard:
  1. Basic business info
  2. Store name & description
  3. Business address
  4. Contact details
  5. Category selection
  6. GSTIN / registration number
  7. PAN card
  8. Bank account details (IFSC)
  9. Store photos
  10. Operating hours
  11. Delivery radius
  12. Terms acceptance

### Step 3: Verification (Days 5-7)
- Verify business documents
- Verify bank account (small deposit test)
- Verify store location (physical visit or Google Maps)
- Approve seller account

### Step 4: Catalog Upload (Days 7-10)
- Train seller on product listing
- Help photograph first 20 products
- Set prices and inventory counts
- Verify products are searchable

### Step 5: Training (Days 10-12)
- Order acceptance workflow
- Inventory management
- Customer communication
- Packing guidelines
- Dispute handling

### Step 6: Go Live (Day 12+)
- Enable store for customers
- Monitor first 5 orders closely
- Provide real-time support

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | Minimum |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Sellers onboarded | 10+ | 5 |
| Products per seller | 30+ | 20 |
| Verification rate | 90%+ | 80% |
| First order within 7 days | 80% | 60% |
| Seller satisfaction | 4.0+/5 | 3.5/5 |
| Fulfillment rate | 95%+ | 85% |

---

## Common Issues & Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "I don't have product photos" | Offer to photograph products for them |
| "I can't manage inventory" | Start with top 20 best-sellers only |
| "I can't deliver" | Start with store pickup, add delivery later |
| "Technology is hard" | Provide 1-on-1 training session |
| "What's the cost?" | Free during pilot, explain commission model |

---

## Feedback Collection

- Daily check-in WhatsApp message (first week)
- Weekly phone call (after first week)
- In-app feedback prompt after each order
- Exit interview if seller churns
