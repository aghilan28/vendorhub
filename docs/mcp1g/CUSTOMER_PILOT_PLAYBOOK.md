# Customer Pilot Playbook

**Target:** 25-100 customers in pilot geography  
**Timeline:** Week 2-4 of pilot (after sellers are active)  

---

## Acquisition Strategy

### Channel 1: Personal Network (Week 2)
- Friends, family, campus community
- Direct WhatsApp messages with link
- "Try ordering from [store name] on VendorHub"
- Target: 10-20 early adopters

### Channel 2: Local Community (Week 2-3)
- Apartment complex notice boards
- Local WhatsApp groups
- College campus groups
- "Free delivery on first order" or "₹50 off first order"
- Target: 15-30 customers

### Channel 3: Seller Network (Week 3-4)
- Sellers share with their existing customers
- "Now order online from [store name]"
- QR code in-store pointing to the app
- Target: 10-30 customers

### Channel 4: Social Media (Week 3-4)
- Instagram/WhatsApp stories
- "Hyperlocal delivery in [neighborhood]"
- Target: 10-20 customers

---

## Customer Journey (First Order)

1. **Discover** — Receives link or sees poster → visits VendorHub
2. **Register** — Quick sign-up (email + phone)
3. **Browse** — Sees nearby stores and products
4. **Select** — Adds items to cart
5. **Checkout** — Enters address, selects delivery/pickup
6. **Pay** — Razorpay payment (UPI/card/COD)
7. **Track** — Sees order status updates
8. **Receive** — Gets delivery or picks up
9. **Review** — Prompted for feedback after delivery

---

## Feedback Loop

| Timing | Method | Questions |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| After first order | In-app modal | "How was your experience? (1-5)" |
| Day 3 | WhatsApp message | "Did you find what you needed?" |
| Day 7 | NPS survey | "Would you recommend VendorHub?" (0-10) |
| Day 14 | Phone call (for churners) | "Why didn't you order again?" |
| Day 30 | Satisfaction survey | 5-question survey |

---

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | Minimum |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Registered users | 50+ | 25 |
| First order conversion | 50%+ | 30% |
| Repeat customers (within 14 days) | 30%+ | 15% |
| Customer satisfaction | 4.0+/5 | 3.5/5 |
| NPS | 40+ | 20 |
| Support tickets per order | <0.2 | <0.4 |

---

## Incentives (Pilot Budget)

| Incentive | Cost | Purpose |
|-----------|------|---------|
| First order discount (₹50 off) | ₹50/customer | Activation |
| Free delivery (first 3 orders) | ₹30-50/order | Trial |
| Referral reward (₹25 each) | ₹50/referral | Growth |
| Review reward (₹10 cashback) | ₹10/review | Feedback |

**Estimated pilot budget:** ₹5,000-15,000 for 50-100 customers

---

## Red Flags to Watch

| Signal | Threshold | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| No repeat orders after 14 days | <10% repeat | Interview churned users |
| High cancellation rate | >15% | Investigate causes |
| Low satisfaction | <3.0/5 | Immediate user interviews |
| Support overload | >0.5 tickets/order | Fix top issues immediately |
| Payment failures | >5% | Check Razorpay integration |
